What should a researcher check?
Sensor contact and placement are consistent
Timestamp and sampling frequency are aligned
Movement artefacts are identified and flagged
Missing segments are handled using a pre-defined rule
A clean-looking waveform is not automatically a meaningful physiological measurement. Signal quality and physiological validity are two different questions.

My research principle
Don't optimise for a convincing result.
Optimise for a result you can trust.

A good workflow makes it easy to notice when data contradicts the hypothesis, document uncertainty, and decide what experiment should happen next.
